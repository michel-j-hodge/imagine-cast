Palm Flame

Cast in three steps: make a fist, show a sign, then open your hand. Close your fist to end the spell.

  • ✌️ Index and middle up: flame
  • 🤘 Index and pinky up: lightning
  • 👌 Thumb and index pinched: spell circle
  • ☝️ Index finger up: ball of light

Ball of light: flick your hand up to toss it, then catch it with an open hand.
